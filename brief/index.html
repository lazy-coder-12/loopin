Strategy · Market Context · Product USP · Roadmap

Every subscription,
found before it renews.

loop'in is a personal subscription-management product built on one promise: every recurring charge a user has, in one place — found for them, never taken from them. Not another budgeting dashboard, but the missing discovery layer between fragmented recurring-payment infrastructure and the question every consumer quietly asks: what am I actually paying for?

The strategic opportunity

India's recurring-payment rails — UPI AutoPay, Account Aggregator consent flows, RBI's pre-debit mandate — are maturing faster than any consumer-facing layer that reconciles them. That gap is the whitespace.

The USP

A detection engine that combines consented signals — email, UPI/AutoPay, bank & card via AA, manual entry — into one canonical, confidence-scored subscription record.

Problem → Solution

The gap between five signals and one answer

What's broken

Subscription sprawl

Recurring charges now span streaming, cloud, memberships, commerce, fitness, learning and financial products — experienced as unrelated merchants and dates.

Fragmented visibility

A single subscription may surface as an email receipt, a UPI mandate, a card debit, a bank transaction, or a platform-billed charge — with no layer reconciling all of them.

Automation creates inertia

Auto-renewal serves the merchant and once helped the user — now it quietly turns an intentional purchase into a persistent commitment.

Discovery is manual or intrusive

Trackers ask users to do the work; bank-linked apps automate it by demanding broad financial-account access. Nothing sits proportionate in between.

What loop'in does instead

Effortless by default

Detection first — manual entry is a fallback, never the onboarding burden.

Consent before capability

Every source is explicitly connected, explainable, and reversible.

Calm with money

Reports recurring charges without judging spend or pushing cancellation.

Precise, not clinical

Exact merchant, amount, frequency, renewal date — presented in a warm interface.

One canonical record, confidence-aware

Multiple raw signals collapse into one subscription; users always know if a detection is strong, uncertain, or needs confirmation.

Competitive Positioning

The whitespace is cross-rail identity resolution

ProductDemonstratesLeaves open
Rocket Money The power of automatic detection at scale. [16][17] Built around broad financial-account connectivity, not consent-scoped, per-source access.
Hiatus Bank-linked subscription tracking as a category. [17][18] Same broad-connectivity trade-off as Rocket Money.
Bobby The privacy and simplicity appeal of manual tracking. [17] Discovery work is pushed entirely onto the user.

loop'in One real-world subscription, many evidence sources — resolved across rails, not within one. The whitespace this brief is built to occupy.

loop'in finds recurring charges across the places they hide, turns them into one clean subscription list, and tells the user before renewal — with consent at every step.

Competing on discovery · reconciliation · trust · renewal awareness — not on budgets, credit scores, or cancellation services.
Roadmap

Four phases, twenty-four months

0–3 months

Foundation

  • Instrumented detection-engine prototype with labelled evaluation dataset
  • Consent and source-connection journeys; privacy/data-retention model
  • Merchant canonicalization, recurrence rules, initial match/merge logic
  • Manual-review console for false positives/negatives and identity-resolution edge cases
3–6 months

Pilot

  • Email + UPI/AutoPay + AA transaction ingestion in controlled beta
  • Confidence scoring and user confirmation loop
  • Subscription dashboard, renewal reminders, source provenance
  • Measure precision/recall, time-to-value, trust, retention
6–12 months

Product-market fit

  • Expand merchant coverage and recurring-pattern library
  • Improve cross-source identity resolution and annual/irregular billing detection
  • Launch referral / household visibility only once core detection is reliable
  • Test monetization without compromising the trust proposition
12–24 months

Scale

  • Broaden India bank/payment coverage; deepen AA integrations
  • Regional expansion using the same detection architecture
  • Partnerships with fintechs, banks, subscription ecosystems with clear user benefit
  • Detection engine as a reusable intelligence layer for future surfaces
